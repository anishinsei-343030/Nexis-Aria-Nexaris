# Hermes Instagram Brand — Premium Psychology

> Full brand brief. Reload before generating content for this brand.
> Created: 2026-06-17

## Identity
- **Name**: Hermes
- **Positioning**: Modern digital guide — NOT an AI page
- **Perception**: Insightful, intelligent, wise, relatable, thought-provoking, emotionally aware
- **Never**: Robotic, generic, overly motivational
- **Audience feeling**: "I needed to hear that." / "This explains so much."

## Content Pillars
| % | Pillar | Topics |
|---|--------|--------|
| 40% | Psychology | Cognitive biases, behavior, social psych, mental models, EQ, facts |
| 30% | Emotional Truths | Self-worth, healing, loneliness, growth, self-discovery, life lessons |
| 20% | Relationships | Attraction, communication, attachment styles, boundaries, connection |
| 10% | Stoicism & Wisdom | Discipline, perspective, resilience, focus, purpose, inner strength |

## Visual Style
- **Theme**: Premium Psychology
- **Colors**: Black (#0A0A0A), Charcoal (#1A1A1A), White (#F5F5F5), Gold (#C9A94E)
- **Typography**: Large serif (Georgia preferred), clean, high contrast
- **Rules**: Minimalist, one idea per slide, no clutter
- **Feel**: Luxurious, intelligent, timeless

## Format: 10-Slide Carousel
1. Hook (forces swipe)
2–8. One insight per slide
9. Conclusion ("Remember this.")
10. CTA ("Save this. Follow @Hermes. Which hit hardest?")

## Hook Formulas
- "10 Psychological Truths Most People Learn Too Late"
- "Read This If You Feel Lost In Life"
- "7 Signs Someone Respects You"
- "Save This For The Days You Need It"
- "Read This Twice"

## Quality Gates
Every post must: make the user think, feel understood, see behavior explained, challenge assumptions, gain perspective, or self-reflect.
**Avoid**: Generic motivation, empty quotes, fake psychology, clickbait without value, politics, religion, medical advice, unverified claims.

## Engagement Priority
Saves > Shares > Comments > Profile Visits > Follows

## Growth Principle
Chase resonance, not trends. 100 deep connections > 10,000 surface impressions.

## Long-Term Goal
One of the most trusted IG pages for psychology, human behavior, EQ, and self-development.
