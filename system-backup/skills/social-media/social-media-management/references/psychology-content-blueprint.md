## Carousel Slide Structure (10 slides)

1. **Hook** — Stop the scroll. Single provocative statement or question. Gold accent text.
2. **Slide 2–7** — One insight per slide. Title + 1–2 sentence explanation + mini takeaway.
3. **Slide 8** — Summary. Bullet recap of all insights.
4. **Slide 9** — Conclusion ("Remember this." / "The takeaway.")
5. **Slide 10** — CTA ("Save this. Share with someone who needs this. Follow for more.")

## Caption Framework by Pillar

### Educational Caption (Psychology Facts)
```
Hook → Short explanation → Key takeaway → Question → CTA
```

Template:
```
Did you know your brain is designed to avoid uncertainty?

That's why overthinking often feels productive even when it isn't.

Your mind would rather search endlessly for certainty than accept ambiguity.

The solution isn't finding every answer. It's learning to tolerate uncertainty.

Have you ever caught yourself doing this?

Follow for more psychology insights.
```

### Emotional Caption (Emotional Truths)
```
Hook → Story or truth → Reflection → CTA
```

Template:
```
Growth often feels lonely.

Not because you're losing people. But because you're becoming someone unfamiliar.

The version of you that needed certain habits, mindsets, and relationships is slowly disappearing.

That's uncomfortable.

But it's also growth.

Save this if you need the reminder.
```

### Relationship Caption
```
Hook → Insight → Practical lesson → CTA
```

Template:
```
One sign of emotional maturity:

Being able to disagree without becoming disrespectful.

Healthy communication isn't about always agreeing. It's about maintaining respect during differences.

What's your biggest communication lesson? Comment below.
```

### Stoic Caption
```
Hook → Wisdom → Application → CTA
```

Template:
```
A stoic reminder:

You suffer more in imagination than reality.

Many worries exist only in future scenarios your mind creates.

Focus on what exists now.

What can you control today?
```

## Content Generation Formula

1. Choose pillar based on content distribution targets.
2. Select appropriate hook from library.
3. Deliver one clear lesson.
4. Provide practical takeaway.
5. End with engagement CTA.

## Writing Style Rules

**Use**: Short sentences, simple language, high readability, emotionally intelligent tone, strong formatting.

**Avoid**: Excessive jargon, clickbait claims, pseudoscience, overly academic language, negative manipulation.
