### Topic 9: Why habits fail

#### Facebook Caption

**Hook:**
Habits fail because of a **dopamine gap.**

**Story/Insight:**
Your brain needs **instant** dopamine after a habit cue. If the reward is delayed (e.g., "I’ll feel better in 3 months"), the loop breaks. That’s why diets and gym routines fail.

習慣が失敗するのは、ドーパミンのギャップ。

**Lesson:**
Make the reward **immediate.** Even if it’s small.
